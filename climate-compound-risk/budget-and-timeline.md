# Budget and Timeline

Ceiling USD 15,000 over 12 months. Budgeted USD 14,500.

The envelope binds far less here than on a primary collection study. This is a desk study on open data, so the money goes to time, compute and dissemination rather than fieldwork.

## Budget

| Line | Detail | USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Principal investigator stipend | 12 months part-time. ACET states the grant covers a living stipend | 4,500 |
| Research assistant | 6 months part-time, EM-DAT and DesInventar harmonisation and event matching | 2,800 |
| Cloud compute and storage | Reanalysis retrieval, footprint construction, calibration runs | 1,400 |
| Open access publication | Article processing charge, NHESS or equivalent | 2,000 |
| Expert elicitation | Virtual session to test the exclusion restrictions with loss-database practitioners | 500 |
| Conference | One regional climate or disaster risk conference presentation | 1,600 |
| Policy roundtable | Accra, with finance ministry and NADMO participation | 800 |
| Software, data, miscellaneous | | 200 |
| Contingency | | 700 |
| **Total** | | **14,500** |

Data costs are zero by design. EM-DAT is open for non-commercial use, DesInventar is open, reanalysis and satellite precipitation products are open, and CLIMADA with its exposure layers is open source.

## Timeline

| Months | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Harmonise EM-DAT and DesInventar for African countries; build the event-matching layer; **verify DesInventar coverage**, which is the go or no-go check |
| 3 to 4 | Construct compound hazard footprints for drought-then-flood and concurrent heat-drought; assemble exposure and poverty layers |
| 5 | Model the reporting process; specify and test candidate exclusion restrictions |
| 6 to 8 | Calibrate impact functions twice, conventionally and corrected; estimate the bias by income group and hazard sequence |
| 9 | Translate the estimated bias into implications for finance allocated on reported losses |
| 10 | Working paper draft; review with ACET Senior Fellow mentor |
| 11 | Expert validation; Accra policy roundtable |
| 12 | Journal submission, open release of corrected impact functions, conference |

The critical path runs through months 1 and 2. If DesInventar coverage is thin, the fallback in the design note applies and the schedule holds, but the claim narrows.
