# Compound Climate Risk and Loss Reporting Bias

Second concept for the ACET Young Leaders in Research 2027 cohort. Alternative to the food processing proposal in `../acet-ylr-2027/`.

## Deadline

**7 September 2026.** Verify on the official page, which is unreachable from this environment.

## The idea in one line

Impact functions are calibrated on reported disaster losses, reporting completeness rises with national income, so modelled vulnerability is biased downward in the poorest countries and the bias propagates into the risk estimates that adaptation finance is allocated against.
