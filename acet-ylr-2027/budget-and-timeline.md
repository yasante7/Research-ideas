# Budget and Timeline

Ceiling: **USD 15,000** over 12 months. Budgeted: **USD 14,300**, leaving USD 700 unallocated headroom above the line-item contingency.

Rates are planning estimates for Ghanaian field research and should be replaced with quotes from your institution or field partner before submission.

## Budget

| Line | Detail | USD |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ethics clearance | Institutional review board, Ghanaian institution | 400 |
| Instrument development | Survey design, translation (Twi, Fante, Ewe, Dagbani), CAPI programming | 500 |
| Pilot | 2 days, 4 enumerators, ~40 interviews, instrument revision | 600 |
| Enumerator training | 3 days, 10 enumerators + 2 supervisors | 800 |
| Survey fieldwork — enumerators | 10 × 12 days × 30 | 3,600 |
| Survey fieldwork — supervisors | 2 × 14 days × 50 | 1,400 |
| Transport and fuel | 2 vehicles, coastal and inland rounds | 1,600 |
| Accommodation and per diem | Field team, 14 days | 1,500 |
| Devices and connectivity | Tablet rental, data bundles | 400 |
| Qualitative fieldwork | 40 in-depth interviews, transcription, translation | 1,000 |
| Research assistant | Data cleaning and analysis support, 6 weeks part-time | 900 |
| Respondent tokens | 500 respondents, nominal compensation | 300 |
| Dissemination | Policy brief production, Accra validation workshop | 600 |
| Contingency | ~5% | 700 |
| **Total** | | **14,300** |

Fieldwork is 58% of the budget, which is the right shape for a primary-collection proposal — reviewers are wary of budgets dominated by the principal investigator's own time.

## What is deliberately not budgeted

- **Equipment.** No solar dryers, ovens, refrigeration or processing machinery are purchased or distributed. This is the single largest departure from the originating advice and the reason the study fits the envelope.
- **PI salary.** Check the call's rules on personal stipend. If salary is permitted and you need it, it comes out of the USD 700 headroom plus trimming the Northern component — not out of fieldwork.

## Timeline

| Months | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Ethics clearance; instrument design and translation; sampling frame assembly from distributor and programme lists |
| 3 | Pilot; instrument revision; enumerator recruitment and training |
| 4–5 | Main survey fieldwork — coastal round, then inland round |
| 6 | Qualitative fieldwork, 40 in-depth interviews |
| 7–8 | Data cleaning, analysis, mixed logit estimation, event-study specification |
| 9–10 | Working paper draft; review with ACET Senior Fellow mentor |
| 11 | Accra validation workshop; policy brief |
| 12 | Revision, journal submission, ACET presentation |

The critical path runs through **ethics clearance and the sampling frame**, both in months 1–2. Slippage there compresses everything downstream, so start the frame conversations before the grant begins if you can.
